# Men's international football results since 1872

Every men's full international match: the home team as sender, the away
team as receiver.

## Usage

``` r
football_results
```

## Format

A data frame with 49,945 rows: `time` (days since 30 November 1872),
`date`, `sender` (home team), `receiver` (away team), `home_score`,
`away_score`, `tournament`, `neutral` (`TRUE` when played at a neutral
venue).
