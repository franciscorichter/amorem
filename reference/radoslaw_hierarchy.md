# Reporting hierarchy of the manufacturing company (Nurek & Michalski 2020)

Who reports to whom among the employees of
[radoslaw_email](https://franciscorichter.github.io/amorem/reference/radoslaw_email.md).
Employee 86 is the chief executive and reports to themself.

## Usage

``` r
radoslaw_hierarchy
```

## Format

A data frame with 167 rows and 2 columns:

- id:

  Character employee id, as in
  [radoslaw_email](https://franciscorichter.github.io/amorem/reference/radoslaw_email.md).

- reports_to:

  Character id of the employee's manager.
